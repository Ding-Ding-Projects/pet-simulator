Terms of service

Written to be read, not skimmed past. These terms are deliberately short.

The game

  • The base game is free and complete. Optional purchases (gem packs, a season pass) buy deterministic items only — never a random outcome.
  • Nothing you buy is ever revoked, expired, or downgraded by an update. A purchase covering one major version covers it forever; a later major version is a separate, opt-in upgrade whose price is stated before you commit.
  • Refunds are always granted — see the refund policy.

Accounts and fair play

  • An account is required only for purchases and online features (cloud saves, leaderboards, trading). Solo play never requires one.
  • Online submissions (scores, trades, listings) are validated server-side, including exact replay verification of submitted progress. Submissions that cannot have happened are rejected.
  • Accounts found manipulating online systems are quarantined: their scores leave leaderboards and their trading freezes, while solo play remains available forever and their purchases are automatically refunded in full. We do not keep money from accounts we quarantine. Every quarantine can be appealed in-app.
  • We never ban an account for requesting refunds, and quarantine never deletes pets, saves, or progress.

Children

Accounts for children under 13 require verifiable parental consent. Under-18 accounts carry a monthly spending cap. Trading confirmations are designed to be readable by children, and lopsided trades warn before executing.

The software

  • The Windows installer is unsigned; Windows will show an unknown-publisher warning. The download page explains exactly what you will see and publishes a SHA-256 checksum for verification.
  • The game is provided as-is, without warranty. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under the consumer law of your country.
  • Modifying your own offline game is yours to do — it is your machine. Online features validate everything server-side instead of policing your computer.

Changes

If these terms change, the change is dated, the history stays published, and no change ever takes away something already bought.

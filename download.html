About the unknown-publisher warning

The installer is unsigned — no code-signing certificate is purchased for this project, deliberately. When you run it, Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC" with an unknown publisher. That warning is expected and does not indicate anything is wrong with the file you downloaded from this page:

  1. Click More info on the SmartScreen dialog.
  2. Check the app name shown, then click Run anyway.
  3. To verify the download first, compare its SHA-256 against the checksum published beside the download button (in PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\MaterialPetSimulator-Setup.exe).

What installs

  • The complete, free base game — playable offline immediately, no account, no sign-up.
  • Automatic updates, downloaded in the background and applied only when you choose to restart. An update never crosses a paid major-version boundary without asking you first.

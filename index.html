Hatch them. Raise them. Actually look after them.

Pet Simulator is a pet collector and a pet care game for Windows, in one. Smash breakables, hatch eggs, chase rarities from Common to Secret, fuse, breed, evolve — and feed, groom, and put to bed the pets you collect, because here they are pets, not inventory. Built with Material Design 3.

Base game free & complete No loot boxes, ever No pet ever dies Plays fully offline Windows 10/11 x64

Development status: the game is in active development and has not been released yet. Nothing is downloadable and nothing can be purchased today. This page exists so you can see exactly what is being built and what it will cost — every claim below describes the shipped design, not a promise of a date.

What the game is

Collect

Eggs with published, provable odds. Seven rarity tiers, mutations, enchants, fusion, breeding with real lineages, and a pet index with set bonuses.

Care

Hunger, happiness, cleanliness, energy and bond. Neglected pets droop; cared-for pets sparkle. Illness is always curable — no pet ever dies, anywhere.

Explore

Zones with bosses, day/night and weather, timed expeditions for your bench, seasonal events, and buildable habitats.

Compete

A full turn-based monster battler (losing just makes pets tired), an endless Tower, provably-fair fixed-seed weekly challenges, and photo mode.

Pricing

The entire base game is free — every base zone, every rarity, rebirth, the whole care system, and a real ending. It is not a demo and never requires an account to play. Paying is optional, and money only ever buys deterministic things: never a random outcome, never an egg roll. Gems are also earnable in-game by playing.

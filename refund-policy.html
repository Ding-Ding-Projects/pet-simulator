Refund policy

Every refund request is granted. No questions, no forms about why, no time limit, and no ban for asking — including repeat requests.

What we sell, and how it is delivered

All products are digital goods for the game Pet Simulator on Windows: gem packs ($1.99, $4.99, $9.99, $19.99) and a season pass ($4.99). Delivery is instant and automatic to the signed-in account that made the purchase.

How to request a refund

  1. In the app: one click on the purchase record in your account screen. This is the fastest route and is deliberately faster than any other.
  2. Through GitHub: open an issue with your order id (never post payment details). A support email ships with the first release.

What a refund does

  • Your money is returned in full through the payment method you used.
  • The gems from that purchase are removed. If you already spent them, your gem balance goes negative and future earned gems pay it down — nothing you made with them (pets, zones, cosmetics) is ever taken away from you.
  • Season pass refunds work the same way: money back, pass track returns to the free track, and everything already unlocked stays yours.

Crypto purchases

Cryptocurrency payments (Coinbase Commerce) cannot be reversed by the processor, so crypto purchases are refunded as store credit of equal value. This is stated at the checkout, before you pay, not discovered afterwards.

Timing

Refunds are typically processed immediately, and always within 48 hours. Where automated review flags unusual account activity, a refund may be delayed — never denied, and accounts involved in serious misuse may lose access to online features (leaderboards, trading) while solo play remains available forever. Every flag can be appealed in-app, and every appeal is read.

Chargebacks

You never need one — our own refund is faster than a card dispute and is always granted. If you open one anyway, we simply provide the payment processor with the purchase and refund-offer records.

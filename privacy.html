Privacy

The short version: playing collects nothing. The whole game runs offline with no account, forever. Data collection begins only if you sign in — and signing in is only needed to spend money or use online features.

Playing without an account

No data leaves your machine. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no identifiers. The game and this website make no third-party requests at all.

If you create an account

  • Sign-in is via Google, Microsoft, Apple, or email. We store an opaque account id, never the provider's raw identifiers; with email sign-in we store your email address and a modern password hash (or a passkey, which we recommend — no password exists at all then).
  • Purchases are processed by Stripe or Coinbase Commerce. Card details never touch our servers. We store the order record needed for delivery and refunds.
  • Cloud saves, leaderboards, trading store the game data those features need, attached to your opaque account id.

Children

The base game is playable by anyone with no account and therefore no data collection. A neutral age gate runs at first launch. Accounts for children under 13 require verifiable parental consent before the account exists or spends anything; under-18 accounts carry a monthly spending cap; every purchase states the real currency amount before charging.

The anti-cheat, in the open

Online features are protected by an integrity system whose defining property is that you can see all of it:

  • Consent first: nothing is sent until you have seen a plain-language list of every field with its live values and agreed. Decline, and the entire game remains playable offline forever.
  • A live transparency panel in settings shows every field with the actual current values on your machine — including the device fingerprint hash and which hardware values feed it — plus a log of every transmission with its full payload.
  • What crosses the wire: a fingerprint hash, progress snapshot numbers, and three true/false verdicts. Never a list of your processes, windows, or software — the payload format structurally cannot carry one.
  • Open source: the entire client-side integrity code is published in a public repository, and the panel links the exact commit your build runs.
  • Honesty note: a device fingerprint computed on your machine is treated as a corroborating signal only — we say this because pretending it is proof would be false.

Your data, your exports

Export and import of your own save data is free for everyone, permanently, and never gated by any purchase. Account deletion removes the account record and its server-side data; local saves are yours and stay on your machine.

Where data lives

Server-side data is held on infrastructure operated by Pet Simulator, with payment processing by Stripe and Coinbase Commerce under their own privacy policies.
